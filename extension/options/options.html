AI & data

  • Token-friendly by design: translations use YouTube's free auto-translate by default; AI only picks key words for the parts you actually watch, and every analysis is cached on your server and shared across all users — the same video section is never paid for twice.
  • Example sentences are generated once per word and reused for every future video.
  • Without login or AI, LEATCH uses a small built-in offline dictionary.
  • Settings sync with your Chrome profile; lessons, words and notes live on your LEATCH server.
